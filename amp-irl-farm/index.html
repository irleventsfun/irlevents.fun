System map

One ecosystem.
Four operating phases.

The tiers are not isolated products. They are progressive ways for a business to enter, prove fit, get help, adopt systems, contribute back, and move into higher-value relationships.

Top of pipeline

The content engine creates demand before the offer asks for it.

There is no single “Million Dollar Idea” campaign here. AMP IRL can run multiple repeatable content formats at once, each designed to create proof, attention, sponsor inventory, qualified leads, and a logical next step.

01 · Founder stories

Real businesses. Real bottlenecks.

Short interviews and operator profiles reveal what a company is trying to grow, what is blocking it, and where AMP IRL can add value.

02 · Live activation content

Turn experiences into media.

Game shows, branded events, tastings, community activations, launches and experiential programs become clips, case studies, sponsor proof and lead-generation assets.

03 · Build in public

Show the work, not just the claim.

Document websites, funnels, AI systems, campaigns, media, events and operational improvements from problem through implementation.

04 · Local business features

Make the network visible.

Highlight venues, brands, operators and partners. Each feature can feed referrals, local discovery, sponsorship, events and future collaborations.

05 · Education + analysis

One idea. One behavior. One next step.

Practical content around marketing, events, sales, AI, customer experience and operations moves viewers from passive attention into implementation.

06 · Partner series

Borrow trust. Create distribution.

Co-produced episodes and recurring series with clients, sponsors and subject-matter partners create new audiences while lowering content acquisition cost.

Operating rule: every piece of content gets a next step. Every next step creates data. Every data point improves the ecosystem. Content can feed T1A, T1B, T1C, a diagnostic, an event, SOS, or a direct sales conversation.

12-month operating rhythm

Build. Validate. Scale what proves itself.

The original Farm sequence still works for AMP IRL: front-load the content/product/system build, validate privately, open the proven pieces publicly, then recruit builders and leaders from the people already demonstrating fit.

MONTHS 1–3

BUILD

  • Content engine + repeatable formats
  • T1A product and implementation tools
  • Lead capture + data model
  • Community/event infrastructure
  • SOS onboarding path
MONTHS 4–6

ANALYZE + BETA

  • Private T1A implementation cohort
  • Diagnostic offer
  • SOS early-adopter lane
  • T1B community activation
  • Revision + launch gates
MONTHS 7–9

VALIDATE + SCALE

  • Public release of proven offers
  • Publish case studies and proof
  • Expand T1C fulfillment
  • Recruit Tier 3 builders
  • Increase sponsor/media inventory
MONTHS 10–12

BUILDERS + HARVEST

  • Advanced SOS + custom agents
  • Builder community
  • Farmhouse pilot
  • Flagship experiences
  • Test first expansion market

The timeline is an operating sequence, not a promise that every offer must wait for a specific month. Existing AMP IRL capabilities can generate revenue immediately while the ecosystem is being assembled.

The Farm thesis

Every front door should lead into the same AMP IRL ecosystem.

A business might first meet AMP IRL through a video, a game show, a branded activation, a website, a referral, an AI conversation, a community event or a diagnostic. The point is not which door they enter. The point is that the system knows what the next useful step is.
