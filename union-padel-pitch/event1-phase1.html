Cosmic Padel × Rave Bingo · Phase 01

Pre-Production

Make the event sellable before asking people to buy it.

Detailed expectations

  • Lock the 3-hour flow, court rotations, DJ/loft, bingo, VIP and arcade concepts.
  • Build sponsor inventory and begin warm + cold sponsor outreach.
  • Union works warm community relationships; AMP / IRL adds cold and existing-network outreach.
  • Create group-sales incentives, including comp tickets for communities that move an agreed number of tickets.
  • Develop promo creative: normal rally → lights out → neon rally → “Don’t just play padel. Play Cosmic Padel.”
  • Set ticket structure, landing-page story, data-capture goal and technical requirements.

Union

Venue access, member/community relationships, internal marketing, operating constraints and warm partner introductions.

AMP / IRL

Concept architecture, production planning, sponsor packaging, cold outreach, incentives and external promo development.

Shared

Ticket plan, sponsor approvals, brand usage, partner selection, data permissions and success metrics.
