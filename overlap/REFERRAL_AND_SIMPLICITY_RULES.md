# The Overlap — Referral Loop + Simplicity Rules

## Core promise
The Overlap is a measurement tool first.

It helps a person:
1. measure their own values, needs, flexibility, and contribution;
2. compare those settings with another person;
3. understand where the overlap is easy, costly, or unclear;
4. use a small library of tools to meet their own needs better and make clearer agreements.

It is **not** a social network, dating marketplace, therapy platform, group chat, content feed, or lifestyle super-app.

## QR comparison loop
Every authenticated profile receives one stable personal comparison link and corresponding QR code.

Suggested user-facing copy:
- **Scan to find our overlap**
- **Curious where we actually match? Scan this.**
- The QR opens the owner's comparison invitation flow, not the owner's account or private answers.

The owner can revoke/regenerate the QR identity if needed.

## Referral reward
A raw scan earns nothing.

A reward is earned only after a new person completes a valid comparison through the owner's referral/comparison QR.

Default beta rule:
- 1 qualified completed referral comparison = 1 free month of Overlap+
- Maximum 6 earned months in any rolling 12-month period
- Months may stack
- Rewards apply to Overlap+ only, not Overlap Together
- No cash value
- Self-referrals, duplicate/refarmed accounts, abandoned assessments, or manipulated completions do not qualify

The cap is deliberately six, not twelve, during beta so the referral loop can prove acquisition value without eliminating the reason to subscribe.

## What must stay simple
A first-time user should be able to complete the core measurement without reading documentation or contacting support.

### Primary navigation should remain small
Recommended top-level product areas:
- My Overlap
- Compare
- Plans
- Tools
- Account

Together may add a single Shared Space entry when enabled. Do not add feeds, communities, followers, messaging inboxes, public profiles, or endless dashboards.

### Every feature must pass this test
Does it help the user:
- measure something important;
- understand a mismatch;
- meet a need more clearly;
- make or test an agreement; or
- remember/review something they intentionally decided?

If not, it probably does not belong in The Overlap.

## Support-by-design rule
The product should not require a human support team to explain normal use.

Before adding support documentation, first improve the UI so the question is unnecessary.

Use:
- plain-language microcopy;
- examples beside unfamiliar concepts;
- visible privacy/share status;
- obvious undo/revoke/delete controls;
- short contextual explanations instead of a giant help center;
- graceful error messages that tell the user exactly what to do next.

Human support should be reserved for billing, account recovery, privacy/data requests, security issues, and genuine bugs.

## Data philosophy
Store enough history to help the user recognize patterns, but do not create surveillance of the relationship.

No partner leaderboard. No streak-shaming. No hidden relationship score. No public ranking. No algorithm declaring whether people should stay together.

The database exists to remember what the user chose to measure, compare, test, and review.
