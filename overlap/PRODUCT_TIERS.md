# The Overlap — Product Ladder

Tagline: **This is not a dating app.**

The product should grow from self-understanding to connection to shared-life coordination without turning the free product into a generic couples app.

## 1. The Overlap — Free
**Job:** Help me understand my own values, thresholds, flexibility, and recurring sources of friction.

Included:
- Personal profile and the core 12-topic assessment
- Private answer history / current profile
- Basic attachment-pattern reflection without diagnosis
- One active comparison at a time
- Basic overlap visualization
- Standard 14-day starter plan template
- DEAR MAN preparation
- Conversation timer
- Core educational/resource library
- Manual check-ins

Free should be useful by itself and viral enough that a user naturally invites another person.

## 2. Overlap+ — Better Connections
**Job:** Help me learn from multiple relationships and deliberately improve how I connect.

Included beyond Free:
- Multiple active comparisons and comparison history
- Profile/version history
- Advanced overlap explanations and friction patterns
- Saved private notes and reflection history
- Personalized 14-day plans instead of the standard template
- Plan reminders and scheduled reviews
- Daily/weekly connection prompts
- Personal mood/capacity tracking
- Prompt and plan notification preferences
- Deeper resource/method recommendations
- Exportable relationship/connection summaries controlled by the user

Overlap+ remains individual-first. The account owner controls what is shared.

## 3. Overlap Together — Shared Life
**Job:** Help two or more people understand each other, coordinate real life, and build habits together.

Designed for:
- Couples
- Families and blended families
- Co-parents
- Roommates / chosen-family households
- Caregiving households
- Other small shared-life groups

Included beyond Overlap+:
- Shared space for multiple members with roles/permissions
- Shared mood and capacity pulses with opt-in visibility
- Shared daily/weekly intimacy/connection prompts
- Custom household or relationship 14-day plans
- Shared goals, rituals, agreements, and review dates
- Shared planning/calendar layer
- Household responsibilities / ownership tracking
- Family or household values map showing areas of common ground and friction
- Shared decision records (what we agreed, who owns what, review date)
- Push/email notification preferences per member
- Optional private areas that never become visible merely because someone joins a household

## Product boundary
The Overlap is not a dating marketplace, therapy service, diagnostic tool, safety assessment, or productivity suite. Shared-life features exist only when they reinforce the product's core promise: **make invisible expectations visible, test workable agreements, and reduce the cost of guessing what other people need.**

## Upgrade logic
Free -> "Understand yourself."
Overlap+ -> "Build better connections."
Overlap Together -> "Build a life that works together."

Do not lock safety, consent, deletion, privacy, or access-to-own-data controls behind payment.
