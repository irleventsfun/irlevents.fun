The Overlap

This is not a dating app.

It is what you use when attraction is no longer the interesting question.

The Overlap shows where two real lives naturally fit, where either person can flex without disappearing, and which differences deserve an honest conversation before they become the same fight in a different outfit.

For couples, people getting serious, and anyone who would rather test daily-life fit than guess at it.

The middle is not the goal.A healthy relationship does not require two people to become identical. The useful question is where your needs overlap naturally, where flexibility is easy, and where compromise starts costing too much.
Relationship fit, without the verdict

Stop asking “Are we compatible?” Ask “What would daily life cost us?”

The Overlap does not hand out a compatibility score or tell you whether to stay together. It turns invisible assumptions into visible ranges, then gives you a way to test one difficult area for 14 days using actual behavior instead of promises.

01

Tell the truth

Answer from your actual habits and needs—not your best date-night self or the person you think you should be.

02

See the overlap

Compare ideal settings, flexibility, effort, and ownership. A difference can be large but easy; a small difference can still become exhausting.

03

Test the real world

Choose one friction point, make a small 14-day agreement, check capacity, prepare hard conversations, and review what actually happened.

◎

Your relationship has a default temperature.

Each person has a natural setting for closeness, planning, money, family, sex, chores, social energy and more. The person least comfortable with the current setting often asks for change first. The Overlap helps distinguish a reasonable adjustment from one person slowly becoming responsible for everybody’s comfort.

Standalone beta

Built for private reflection, not public performance.

No swiping. No profiles to browse. No public compatibility leaderboard. Private answers stay private until the comparison rules are satisfied, and sensitive topics can be skipped.
